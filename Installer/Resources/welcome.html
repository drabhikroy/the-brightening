Welcome to The Brightening

The Brightening raises the brightness of a MacBook Pro Liquid Retina XDR display above its usual limit. This installer puts the app in your Applications folder and opens it in the menu bar.

If a copy is already running, the installer asks it to quit first so it can put the display back to normal. It opens the new copy when the installation finishes.

Click Continue to choose where to install, or Go Back at any step to change your mind.
